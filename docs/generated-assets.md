# Generated character assets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on November 9, 2026. Both source PNGs retain their alpha channel and are copied into the project; original jet and UFO assets were not overwritten. The four farmer variants are rendered from equal quadrants of one sheet. The mothership's transparent padding is cropped at render time, preserving the PNG.

- `frontend/public/images/characters/farmers-v1.png`: 1254 × 1254 RGBA; red/yellow/blue/purple farmers in a 2 × 2 sheet.
- `frontend/public/images/characters/mothership-v1.png`: 2172 × 724 RGBA; wide parent UFO.

## Farmer prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production game sprite sheet, one square raster PNG with a genuinely transparent background. Primary request: replace jet-plane enemies in a cheerful cow-abduction board game with SHORT, VERY SQUAT cartoon farmers in overalls, each holding an upright three-tined pitchfork. Style: simple friendly flat 2D cartoon, bold clean silhouettes and minimal broad cel shading, matching a little white spotted cartoon cow and flat colored flying saucer game sprites. Slight three-quarter front view; full body; oversized round head, broad belly, very short legs, boots, straw hat, moustache. No scenery, ground shadows, labels, borders or lettering. Composition: EXACTLY FOUR same-size full-body variants in a strict 2 by 2 grid of equal square cells. Top-left RED shirt/hat ribbon accents; top-right YELLOW accents; bottom-left BLUE accents; bottom-right PURPLE accents. All wear denim blue overalls and hold a brown wooden-handled pitchfork with a silver fork. Same character, identical pose, scale and alignment in all four cells; centered with generous transparent margin in each cell. Pitchfork must fit entirely within its own cell. Keep tiny details sparse so each character remains recognizable when its complete cell is scaled down to 40 x 40 pixels. Transparent background everywhere between and around characters. No UI, no text, no extra props.

## Mothership prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent cartoon game sprite, a single very wide mothership UFO viewed slightly from the front, isolated with no background. Match a simple flat 2D cow-abduction game where little UFOs have smooth oval colored saucer bodies, white domes and small white circular lights. This is the parent mothership: a very broad pastel-blue/silver flying saucer, a smooth white central cockpit dome, a restrained pink middle band and several evenly spaced pale yellow round lights. Friendly simple flat shapes, clean silhouette, only minimal broad cel shading, bold enough for a game. Composition: horizontal panoramic spacecraft nearly edge-to-edge, centered, little empty padding, complete hull visible, no cropping; much wider than tall, about 5:1 saucer-body proportion. The underside is a continuous broad flat curved receiver edge so thin cow-transport beams can meet it anywhere across its width. No beams baked into the sprite, no cows, no baby UFOs, no ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, no text, no watermark. Genuinely transparent background. This asset will descend above the entire width of a square game board and change color in CSS; keep its outline and lights crisp.
